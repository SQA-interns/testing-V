# REQ-REG-01 Registration of a conference participant

## Story
As a *participant* of the conference, I want to submit a *registration* with my participant and invoicing data, so that my attendance is recorded and the organizer can invoice the *fee* after the conference.

## Glossary
| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| participant | The natural person who will attend the conference, identified by first name, last name and e-mail address. |
| payer | The party to which the invoice will be addressed: the participant as a private person, or a company (name, address, VAT ID). |
| registration | One stored submission of the registration form, with status `registered`. A participant may have more than one registration (Q2). |
| registration number | Unique identifier of a registration, generated by the system. Format not prescribed. |
| submission time | Server time at which a valid registration request is received (decision Q1). |
| early-bird deadline | Last calendar day of the early-bird period, inclusive, in the conference time zone: `APP_EARLY_BIRD_DEADLINE` (31 July 2026). |
| fee | Conference fee for one registration. Covers the conference, workshops, proceedings and meals. Always stated as net fee, VAT and gross fee. |
| net fee | `APP_FEE_EARLY` (240.00 EUR) if the submission time is on or before the early-bird deadline, otherwise `APP_FEE_REGULAR` (300.00 EUR). |
| VAT | Slovenian VAT at `APP_VAT_RATE` (22 %) on the net fee, for every payer, including companies from other EU member states (place of supply is where the event takes place). |
| gross fee | Net fee plus VAT. |
| workshop | One of the conference workshops listed in `APP_WORKSHOPS`. Workshops have no capacity limit. |

## Acceptance criteria

**AC1 Early-bird fee**
- Given the *submission time* is on or before the *early-bird deadline*
- When a valid *registration* is submitted
- Then the registration is stored with *net fee* 240.00 EUR, *VAT* 52.80 EUR and *gross fee* 292.80 EUR.

**AC2 Regular fee**
- Given the *submission time* is after the *early-bird deadline*
- When a valid *registration* is submitted
- Then the registration is stored with *net fee* 300.00 EUR, *VAT* 66.00 EUR and *gross fee* 366.00 EUR.

**AC3 Deadline boundary**
- Given the conference time zone is Europe/Ljubljana
- When registrations are submitted at 2026-07-31 23:59:59 and at 2026-08-01 00:00:00 local time
- Then the first is stored with the early-bird *net fee* and the second with the regular *net fee*.

**AC4 Confirmation**
- Given a *registration* has been stored
- When the request completes
- Then the API returns 201 with the *registration number*, and exactly one confirmation e-mail is sent to the *participant*, stating the registration number, *net fee*, *VAT* and *gross fee*.

**AC5 Payer data without invoice**
- Given the *payer* is a company
- When a valid *registration* is submitted
- Then the company's name, address and VAT ID are stored with the registration.
- Given the *payer* is the participant as a private person
- When a valid *registration* is submitted
- Then the registration is stored without company data.
- In both cases no invoice and no payment request are created.

**AC6 VAT for payers from other EU member states**
- Given the *payer* is a company from another EU member state
- When a valid *registration* is submitted
- Then *VAT* is charged at the Slovenian rate, with the same amounts as in AC1 and AC2.

**AC7 Validation**
- Given a required field is missing or invalid, or more than one *workshop* is selected
- When a *registration* is submitted
- Then the API returns 422 with one error per field, nothing is stored and no e-mail is sent.
- Required fields: `firstName`, `lastName` and `email` of the *participant*; `payerType` (`private` or `company`); for a company *payer* also `companyName`, `companyAddress` and `companyVatId`.

**AC8 Workshop selection**
- Given the *participant* selects one *workshop* or none
- When a valid *registration* is submitted
- Then the registration is stored with that workshop or with no workshop, and no capacity is checked.

**AC9 Repeated registration (decision Q2)**
- Given a *registration* with the same *participant* e-mail already exists
- When a valid registration is submitted
- Then a second registration is stored with its own *registration number* and *fee*, and the existing registration is left unchanged.

## Non-goals
- NG1 Student registration (students of the organizing faculty, free of charge): separate form, US-002. Do not add a student category or discount.
- NG2 Cancellation or change of a registration: US-003.
- NG3 Check-in, change of workshop at check-in, payment and invoicing: US-004 and the accounting system. Invoices are issued after the conference by the accounting system, only for participants recorded as present at check-in. Do not integrate a payment provider and do not create invoices.
- NG4 Detecting, merging or removing repeated registrations (Q2, Q3).
- NG5 Changes to the configured fee, deadline, VAT or workshop values.

## Open questions
| ID | Question | Status | Owner | Decision, assumption or effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Which point in time determines the fee: submission, e-mail confirmation or payment? | resolved | Organizing committee | Submission time (see Glossary). |
| Q2 | What happens when the same participant registers more than once? | resolved | Organizing committee | Every registration is kept (AC9). |
| Q3 | If a participant has registrations with different fees, which fee is invoiced? | assumed | Organizing committee | Each registration stores its own fee and receives its own confirmation (AC4). The choice of the invoiced fee belongs to invoicing (NG3). |
| Q4 | Must the participant acknowledge the privacy notice when registering? | assumed | Organizing committee | No acknowledgment field. Personal data are handled as defined in `project/02_design/security-requirements.md`. |

Any other implementation decision that cannot be derived from this artifact or from the project-level files is recorded as a blocking decision and is not implemented until a person has answered it.

## Oracle notes
- VAT = net fee × 0.22, rounded `ROUND_HALF_UP` to 0.01: 240.00 → 52.80 → 292.80; 300.00 → 66.00 → 366.00.
- 2026-07-31 23:59:59 CEST = 2026-07-31 21:59:59 UTC (early bird); 2026-08-01 00:00:00 CEST = 2026-07-31 22:00:00 UTC (regular). An implementation that compares UTC dates passes the first case and fails the second.
- A repeated registration on 2026-08-05 by a participant first registered on 2026-07-20 yields two registrations, with net fees 240.00 and 300.00.

- Precedence: configured values (`project/00_setup/environments.md`) prevail over values quoted in this artifact. The quoted values are the expected configuration for 2026 and serve as test expectations.
